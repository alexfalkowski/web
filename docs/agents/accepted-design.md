# Accepted design

This index records Web's accepted design choices and their scope. The area
files carry the same weight as [AGENTS.md](../../AGENTS.md).

Read the relevant area files before proposing a change or recording a finding.
Revisit a decision when the owner asks or new evidence changes its impact.
Record owner-agreed choices and tradeoffs in the owning area file.

- [Site](accepted-design/site.md) covers shared metadata, embedded asset
  caching, and browser asset policy.
- [Testing](accepted-design/testing.md) covers the authoritative product test
  harness and the role of Go specs.
